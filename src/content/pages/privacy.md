_Last updated November 12, 2026_

This blog counts its visits to know which posts are read and where readers come from. It uses [Umami](https://umami.is), which I run on my own server: no cookie, nothing stored in your browser, no advertising, no tracking from one site to another, and no data sold or shared.

## What is collected

- **On each page you open:** the page's address and title, the site that led you here, your browser, operating system, type of device, screen size and language, and the country, region and city found from your IP address. Umami uses that address and your browser to tell visits apart, as an identifier that changes every month, and never stores the address itself.
- **A few actions:** a click on a link that leaves the blog (with its address), a click on a share button (with the network), reaching the end of a post, and what you type in the search box, up to 100 characters.

## Where it goes, and for how long

- **Statistics:** my Umami, relayed by Cloudflare from this site's own address, on a server rented from Hostinger International (Cyprus) in Jakarta, Indonesia, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. They are deleted after two years; encrypted backups, stored with Cloudflare, keep them up to six months longer.
- **The site itself:** served by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the pages, under [its privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Email:** if you write to me, I keep the conversation as long as it needs, and delete it on request.

## Legal basis and your rights

Under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation, the statistics rely on my legitimate interest in knowing how the blog is read. You can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, object to it, or complain to your data protection authority, or in Japan to the Personal Information Protection Commission. The statistics hold nothing that ties them to you by name, so I may be unable to find yours.
